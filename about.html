Two providers, one company

Bhekani Abantu Services was formed when two complementary providers, Bhekani and Protection Services, combined their strengths to meet the security and maintenance needs of a fast-changing South Africa.

We are active in the commercial, residential, industrial and corporate sectors. As a 100% South African owned and operated company, we put our success down to a hands-on, proactive approach, and we design a solution around your requirements rather than sell you a fixed package.

Caring is not a marketing strategy, it's what we do.
